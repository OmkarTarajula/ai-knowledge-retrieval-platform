COMPANY LEAVE AND BENEFITS POLICY
1. Annual Earned Leave: Full-time employees receive 24 days of annual earned leave per calendar year.
2. Casual Leave: Employees are granted 12 days of casual leave annually for personal matters.
3. Sick Leave: Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave. A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding 2 consecutive days.
4. Leave Application Procedure: To apply for earned or casual leave, submit a request via the HR Employee Self-Service Portal at least 3 business days in advance. Your reporting manager must approve the request before the leave starts.
5. Difference Between Casual and Sick Leave: Casual leave must be planned and requested in advance, whereas sick leave can be claimed retroactively upon unforeseen medical emergencies.
